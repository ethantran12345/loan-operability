# Booking Entities and Currency Pairs Procedure

## 1 Lending offices

Meridian Commercial Bank (fictional) has three lending offices: London (Europe/London time), New York (America/New_York time) and Toronto (America/Toronto time). A facility term that refers to any lending office is a commitment that every one of the three offices can perform it.

## 2 Booking entities and currency pairs

The New York booking entity books advances in USD only.

The London booking entity books advances in EUR and GBP.

The Toronto booking entity books advances in CAD only.

An advance may be booked only through a booking entity approved for its currency.

## 3 Booking entities and currency pairs - exceptions

By exception, the New York booking entity may book an advance in EUR where that advance is funded through the New York EUR same-day exception window described in the Funding Windows and Limits Procedure (MCB-OPS-204). This exception has been available since 1 June 2025.
