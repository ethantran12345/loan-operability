# Notice Intake and Validation Procedure

## 1 Purpose and scope

This procedure sets out how Meridian Commercial Bank (fictional) receives and validates borrowing notices for syndicated loan facilities where it acts as administrative agent. It applies to every lending office.

## 2 Notice intake and validation

Borrowing notices are accepted for automated processing only through the Agent's electronic portal. The portal rejects a notice unless it carries all four mandatory fields: the facility identifier (facility_id), the principal amount (amount), the currency (currency) and the requested value date (value_date).

A facility agreement that does not name the portal as the delivery channel, or that does not commit the borrower to the four mandatory fields, cannot be confirmed as supported by automated intake and must be referred to Loan Operations.

## 3 Non-portal notice exceptions

A borrowing notice received by email is not processed automatically. Loan Operations must complete callback authentication of an emailed borrowing notice against the authorised-signatory list before the bank acts on it. The service level for this manual step is one (1) Business Day from receipt.

An emailed notice must carry the same four mandatory fields: facility_id, amount, currency and value_date.

Agreeing to email delivery in a facility agreement is an exception. It requires sign-off by Loan Operations and by the Product Owner under the Approvals and Delegated Authority Register (MCB-POL-007) before signing.
