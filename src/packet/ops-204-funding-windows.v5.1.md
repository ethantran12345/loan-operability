# Funding Windows and Limits Procedure

## 1 Scope and route rule

This procedure sets the funding windows through which Meridian Commercial Bank (fictional) can fund an advance under a syndicated loan facility, and the limit, increment and notice cutoff of each window.

A facility term is supportable only if one notice intake channel, one funding window and one booking entity can all be satisfied by the same advance. The limit, currency, settlement basis or cutoff of one window may not be combined with those of another window.

## 2 USD funding

Same-day funding in US dollars (USD) is available up to USD 50,000,000 per advance, in multiples of USD 100,000, where the borrowing notice is received by 10:30 A.M. New York time on the funding date.

## 3 EUR funding

Same-day funding in Euro (EUR) is available up to EUR 25,000,000 per advance, in multiples of EUR 100,000, where the borrowing notice is received by 9:30 A.M. London time on the funding date.

## 4 GBP funding

Same-day funding in sterling (GBP) is available up to GBP 20,000,000 per advance, in multiples of GBP 100,000, where the borrowing notice is received by 9:30 A.M. London time on the funding date.

## 5 Advance-notice funding

Funding on a T+1 basis is available in USD, EUR and GBP up to 500,000,000 per advance in the currency of the advance, in multiples of 100,000, where the borrowing notice is received by 4:00 P.M. London time at least one (1) Business Day before the funding date. This window does not provide same-day settlement.

## 6 EUR funding - New York exception

By exception, same-day funding in Euro (EUR) may be made from New York up to EUR 20,000,000 per advance, in multiples of EUR 100,000, where the borrowing notice is received by 10:30 A.M. New York time on the funding date. This window has been available since 1 June 2025.

Each use of this window requires Treasury approval under a Treasury exception authority that is in force on the funding date. The authority, its limit and its effective dates are recorded in the Approvals and Delegated Authority Register (MCB-POL-007). If no Treasury exception authority is in force, this window cannot be used.
