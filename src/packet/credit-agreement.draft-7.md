# Preamble

## 0.01 Parties

This Senior Secured Revolving Credit Agreement (this "Agreement") is dated as of [date to be inserted at signing] among Northlight Components B.V. (fictional), as Borrower, the Lenders from time to time party hereto, and Meridian Commercial Bank (fictional), as Administrative Agent.

The parties agree as follows.

# Article I. Definitions

## 1.01 Defined Terms

"Administrative Agent" means Meridian Commercial Bank (fictional), in its capacity as administrative agent for the Lenders, acting through one or more of its Lending Offices.

"Advance" means a loan made by the Lenders to the Borrower under Article II.

"Borrowing Notice" means a request by the Borrower for an Advance delivered to the Administrative Agent in accordance with Section 2.02.

"Business Day" means a day, other than a Saturday or Sunday, on which banks are open for general business in London and New York and which is a TARGET settlement day.

"Commitment" means, as to each Lender, its obligation to make Advances in an aggregate principal amount not exceeding the amount set out opposite its name in Schedule 1.

"Facility" means the revolving credit facility made available under this Agreement in an aggregate principal amount of EUR 150,000,000.

"Lending Office" means each of the London, New York and Toronto offices of the Administrative Agent.

## 1.02 Interpretation

Headings are for convenience only. A reference to a Section, Article or Schedule is a reference to a section or article of, or schedule to, this Agreement. Words in the singular include the plural and the reverse.

# Article II. The Facility

## 2.01 Commitments

Subject to the terms of this Agreement, each Lender severally agrees to make Advances to the Borrower in Euro from time to time during the Availability Period, provided that the aggregate principal amount of all Advances outstanding shall not exceed the Facility amount.

## 2.02 Borrowing Notices

(a) Each Advance shall be made upon the Borrower's delivery of a Borrowing Notice to the Administrative Agent.

(b) Each Borrowing Notice shall be irrevocable once delivered and shall bind the Borrower to borrow in accordance with its terms.

(c) A Borrowing Notice shall be deemed validly given if delivered by electronic mail to the Administrative Agent, and the Administrative Agent shall authenticate and act upon any such notice within one Business Day of receipt. Each Borrowing Notice shall specify the Facility, the principal amount, the currency and the requested value date.

<!-- page 3 -->

## 2.03 Same-Day Advances

The Borrower may request Advances for same-day settlement under any of paragraphs (a), (b) and (c) below. Each paragraph applies independently of the others.

(a) The Borrower may, on any Business Day, request an advance denominated in Euro in an aggregate principal amount of up to EUR 40,000,000 for same-day settlement, provided that the related Borrowing Notice is delivered by 11:00 A.M. on such Business Day, and such advance may be made available through any Lending Office of the Administrative Agent.

(b) The Borrower may request an advance denominated in Euro in an aggregate principal amount of up to EUR 20,000,000 for same-day settlement, provided that the related Borrowing Notice, specifying the Facility, the principal amount, the currency and the requested value date, is delivered through the Agent's electronic portal by 9:30 A.M. London time on such Business Day and the advance is booked through the London Lending Office.

(c) The Borrower may request an advance denominated in Euro in an aggregate principal amount of up to EUR 20,000,000 for same-day settlement through the New York Lending Office, provided that the related Borrowing Notice, specifying the Facility, the principal amount, the currency and the requested value date, is delivered through the Agent's electronic portal by 10:00 A.M. New York time on such Business Day.

## 2.04 Use of Proceeds

The Borrower shall apply the proceeds of each Advance toward its general corporate and working capital purposes.

# Article III. Interest and Fees

## 3.01 Interest

Each Advance shall bear interest for each Interest Period at a rate per annum equal to EURIBOR, determined by reference to the applicable screen rate, plus a margin of 2.25 per cent. Interest shall be calculated on the basis of the actual number of days elapsed and a year of 360 days. If EURIBOR is less than zero, it shall be deemed to be zero.

## 3.02 Commitment Fee

The Borrower shall pay to the Administrative Agent, for the account of the Lenders, a commitment fee in Euro at a rate of 0.40 per cent. per annum on the undrawn Commitments, payable quarterly in arrear.

# Article IV. Repayment and Prepayment

## 4.01 Repayment

The Borrower shall repay each Advance on the last day of its Interest Period and shall repay all amounts outstanding under the Facility on the fifth anniversary of the date of this Agreement.

## 4.02 Voluntary Prepayment

The Borrower may prepay any Advance in whole or in part, without premium or penalty, on not less than three Business Days' prior written notice to the Administrative Agent.

# Article V. Conditions Precedent

## 5.01 Conditions to Each Advance

The obligation of each Lender to make an Advance is subject to the conditions that, on the date of the Borrowing Notice and on the proposed funding date, no Default is continuing and the representations in Article VI are true in all material respects.

# Article VI. Representations and Covenants

## 6.01 Representations

The Borrower represents that it is duly organised and validly existing, that it has the power to enter into and perform this Agreement, and that its obligations under this Agreement are legal, valid, binding and enforceable.

## 6.02 Information Covenants

The Borrower shall deliver to the Administrative Agent its audited annual financial statements within 120 days after the end of each financial year and its unaudited quarterly financial statements within 45 days after the end of each financial quarter.

# Article VII. Events of Default

## 7.01 Events of Default

Each of the following is an Event of Default: non-payment of any amount due under this Agreement within three Business Days of its due date; breach of any other obligation that is not remedied within 20 Business Days; any representation being incorrect in any material respect when made; and the insolvency of the Borrower.

# Article VIII. The Administrative Agent

## 8.01 Appointment

Each Lender appoints the Administrative Agent to act as its agent under this Agreement and authorises it to exercise the rights and discretions specifically given to it, together with any incidental rights.

## 8.02 Operational Matters

The Administrative Agent shall administer Borrowing Notices, fundings and payments under this Agreement in accordance with Article II.

# Article IX. Miscellaneous

## 9.01 Amendments

No term of this Agreement may be amended or waived except in writing signed by the Borrower and the Administrative Agent acting on the instructions of the required Lenders.

## 9.02 Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.

## 9.03 Counterparts

This Agreement may be executed in any number of counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
