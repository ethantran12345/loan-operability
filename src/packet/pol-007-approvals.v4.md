# Approvals and Delegated Authority Register

## 1 Purpose

This register records who may approve operational exceptions for syndicated loan facilities, up to what amount, and for what period. An authority may be relied on only while it is in force. No person may approve a request that they raised themselves.

## 2 Loan Operations authority (apr-018)

Loan Operations may approve operational exceptions up to 100,000,000 per transaction. Effective from 1 January 2026. No expiry date.

## 3 Product Owner authority (apr-019)

The Product Owner may approve operational exceptions up to 500,000,000 per transaction. Effective from 1 January 2026. No expiry date.

## 4 Regional Delegate authority (apr-020)

The Regional Delegate could approve operational exceptions up to 1,000,000,000 per transaction. Effective from 1 January 2025. Expired on 30 June 2026 and not renewed.

## 5 Treasury exception authority (apr-021)

Treasury may approve use of the New York EUR same-day exception window up to 25,000,000 per advance. Effective from 15 September 2026. No expiry date. This renewal replaces the authority that expired on 31 August 2026.
